A work in progress. 

Garden as a space that allows to feel and rest, a space that is contested and longed for, lost and taken away. The wall that separates the garden, the wobbly wooden fence that welcomes some and excludes others. Garden as a place of sustenance, safe and impossible. The beauty, the fragrance of it and the keep out sign.
